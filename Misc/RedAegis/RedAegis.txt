Disturbance/Opposite conscience-less type of Aegis Energy, acting as a kind of "residual" material of the anti version of the other Aeges.
Similar to Anti-matter to matter, or Positrons to Electrons.

Can be manifested by the other ones by the decay of overall Aegis Energy, the more being decayed, the more of it being acumulated.
It is inherent to all of the users, and can be used by them for their purposes.
It can be transformed back to their normal Aegis (Green, Blue, Yellow, Purple or Orange) with absolute ease, given their mirrored relation of existence, so the normal Aeges are the true
expression of the users, and this is more of a bonus to close the gaps and overlaps of manifesting them, however, it does directly affect them to fill the aspects they usually lack or 
avoid, so for example:

If the user chooses to base his morality on evil, the Red Aegis version of the person will usually act for good. (This has been disproven before, however belief on this system serves as a guideline
to understand their behaviour). 

It is normally extremely volatile and unstable, so upon first contact, the users had to overcome entire archs of hardships, morally questionable behaviour and constant conflict, until it was """tamed""".
